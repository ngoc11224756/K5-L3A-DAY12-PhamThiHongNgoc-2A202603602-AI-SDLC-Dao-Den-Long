## 3D Assets & Audio
- All 3D models (Mây with conical hat & hanging lantern, Great Lanterns, Fireflies/Motes, Ink Shades, Island terrain, rising sky lanterns) are **procedurally generated in code** using Three.js primitive geometries. No external third-party 3D models or textures are used.
- All sound effects are **procedurally synthesized in browser** via the Web Audio API (`src/render/audio.ts`). No external audio files are used.
